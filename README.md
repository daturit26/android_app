# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile apps and games published on Google Play by the developer "Piano Tiles 3: Music Games, Tiles Hop, EDM Rush" ("we", "us", "our"). It explains what information is collected when you use our apps (the "App"), how it is used, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other directly identifying information through the App.

The App and the third-party services it uses may automatically collect:

- **Device information:** device model, manufacturer, operating system and version, language, country.
- **Identifiers:** Advertising ID and app instance ID.
- **Usage and gameplay data:** songs played, scores, session length, in-app interactions.
- **Diagnostics:** crash logs and performance data.
- **Network information:** IP address (used by ad and analytics providers, e.g. to estimate approximate location).

## 2. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Operate the App and save your game progress on your device.
- Show advertisements, including personalized ads where you have consented.
- Analyze usage to improve songs, gameplay, and performance.
- Detect and fix crashes and technical problems.
- Prevent fraud and abuse.

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |

We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Advertising ID and Your Choices

The Advertising ID is a resettable identifier provided by your device's operating system. You can reset it or opt out of personalized ads at any time:

- **Android:** Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads), then reset your Advertising ID or delete it.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice from the App's settings.

## 5. In-App Purchases

If the App offers in-app purchases, payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App. Data collected by third-party services is retained according to their policies. To request deletion of any data associated with your device, contact us at the email below.

## 7. Data Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
